Personal sessions

Pilates built around you

Reformer and mat Pilates, stretching and hands-on conditioning are combined around your experience and goals. Sessions are private, with a trainer guiding your movements and adjusting the pace.

Whether you are new to exercise or returning after a break, you can work on posture, strength and a routine that fits your life. All menus are available to guests of any gender.

Our staff mainly speak Japanese and use a translation app with English-speaking guests. Please share any questions or concerns when booking.

FAQ

Before your first session

Is this a group Pilates class?
No. CoconeRu. Move offers private, one-to-one sessions. Your trainer adapts reformer or mat Pilates and conditioning to your experience, goals and how you feel that day.
Can beginners book a session?
Yes. Beginners and people returning to exercise are welcome. All menus are available to guests of any gender. A first-visit trial can be selected in the booking calendar.
Can I visit if I do not speak Japanese?
English-speaking guests are welcome. Our staff mainly speak Japanese and use a translation app when needed. Please tell us about your goals and any concerns when booking.
How much does private Pilates cost?
A 60-minute session is JPY 9,000 at the standard rate or JPY 7,000 for Move VIP members. A 90-minute session is JPY 12,000 at the standard rate or JPY 9,500 for Move VIP members. CoconeRu. Pass for Head Spa is a separate membership.
Where is CoconeRu. Move?
The studio is at 2F Yashima Building, 2-11 Otaki-cho, Yokosuka, Kanagawa 238-0008, a 4-minute walk from Keikyu Yokosuka-chuo Station. It is a different address from Head Spa. Hours are 10:00-20:00, with irregular closing days; please check availability before visiting.
