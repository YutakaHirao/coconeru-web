What to expect

Massage & Head Spa

Dry head spa, body care and facials

Choose a Japanese dry head spa, body care for your neck and shoulders, or a facial-and-head combination. Standard treatments last 60 minutes, followed by 20 minutes of quiet rest. Standard-rate bookings and CoconeRu. Pass memberships are available.

The dry head spa is a head and scalp massage given with no water, no shampoo and no oil, so you stay dressed, your hair stays dry, and you can walk straight back out into your day. In a low-lit room we ease tension through the head, around the eyes and across the neck and shoulders. It is relaxation rather than medical or clinical treatment, and it suits anyone carrying screen fatigue, tired eyes or a stiff neck at the end of a long week. Head Spa is on the 3rd floor of First Building, 3-4 Wakamatsu-cho, Yokosuka.

Menus and prices

Move

Personal Training & Pilates

CoconeRu. Move is a one-to-one personal training studio in a bright, friendly space, pairing machine (reformer) and mat pilates with hands-on conditioning to help you move and sit more comfortably.

Sessions are private and built around you, whether your goal is better posture, easing a stiff neck or shoulders, building a steady exercise habit, or working gradually toward a fitter, stronger body. Beginners and people returning to exercise after a long break are very welcome, and guests of all genders are welcome. There is no pressure to push hard, only to keep going at a pace that lasts. Move is on the 2nd floor of Yashima Building, 2-11 Otaki-cho, Yokosuka, and a first-visit trial session is available.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Common questions from English-speaking guests in Yokosuka. There are more on the first visit page.

Is there a massage place near Yokosuka Naval Base?
Yes. CoconeRu. is a quiet, appointment-only massage and dry head spa salon in central Yokosuka, roughly 15 minutes on foot from Womble Gate and about 4 minutes from Keikyu Yokosuka-chuo Station. Body care works the neck, shoulders and back; the dry head spa is a Japanese head and scalp massage given without water or oil. Hours: 10:00–20:00; irregular closing days at 3F First Building, 3-4 Wakamatsu-cho, Yokosuka.
How much does a massage cost at CoconeRu.?
Standard visits include 60 minutes of treatment plus 20 minutes of rest; 90-minute treatments are also available. Standard rates are ¥7,500 for a dry head spa or body 30 min + head 30 min, ¥8,500 with hydrogen-water cleansing, and ¥11,000 for 90 minutes. Members pay ¥4,700 to ¥4,933 per visit. All prices include tax, and there is no tipping in Japan. See the full price list and membership.
Do you accept English-speaking customers?
Yes. Our staff speak mainly Japanese and use a translation app with English-speaking guests, which works well in practice. Booking online is easiest because you pick the menu and time on screen without a phone call. If you prefer a quieter session, please let us know when booking.
Do you take credit cards or US dollars?
We accept credit cards, cash in Japanese yen, and QR payments such as PayPay. We cannot accept US dollars, so bring yen if you plan to pay in cash. Tipping is not customary in Japan and is never expected.
Do you offer personal training or pilates in Yokosuka?
Yes. CoconeRu. Move is a private, one-to-one personal training and pilates studio at 2F Yashima Building, 2-11 Otaki-cho, a 4-minute walk from Yokosuka-chuo Station. Sessions combine machine (reformer) and mat pilates with hands-on conditioning for posture, stiff shoulders and a lasting exercise habit. Beginners and guests of all genders are welcome, and a first-visit trial is available.
