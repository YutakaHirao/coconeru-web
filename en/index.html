What to expect

Massage & Head Spa

Body care and Japanese dry head spa

Two things happen here. Body care is hands-on work through the neck, shoulders and back — the ordinary massage most people are after. The dry head spa is a Japanese speciality you will not easily find back home.

The dry head spa is a head and scalp massage given with no water, no shampoo and no oil, so you stay dressed, your hair stays dry, and you can walk straight back out into your day. In a low-lit room we ease tension through the head, around the eyes and across the neck and shoulders. It is relaxation rather than medical or clinical treatment, and it suits anyone carrying screen fatigue, tired eyes or a stiff neck at the end of a long week. Head Spa is on the 3rd floor of First Building, 3-4 Wakamatsu-cho, Yokosuka.

Menus and prices

Move

Personal Training & Pilates

CoconeRu. Move is a one-to-one personal training studio in a bright, friendly space, pairing machine (reformer) and mat pilates with hands-on conditioning to help you move and sit more comfortably.

Sessions are private and built around you, whether your goal is better posture, easing a stiff neck or shoulders, building a steady exercise habit, or working gradually toward a fitter, stronger body. Beginners and people returning to exercise after a long break are very welcome, and both men and women train here. There is no pressure to push hard, only to keep going at a pace that lasts. Move is on the 2nd floor of Yashima Building, 2-11 Otaki-cho, Yokosuka, and a first-visit trial session is available.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Common questions from English-speaking guests in Yokosuka. There are more on the first visit page.

Is there a massage place near Yokosuka Naval Base?
Yes. CoconeRu. is a quiet, appointment-only massage and dry head spa salon in central Yokosuka, roughly 15 minutes on foot from Womble Gate and about 4 minutes from Keikyu Yokosuka-chuo Station. Body care works the neck, shoulders and back; the dry head spa is a Japanese head and scalp massage given without water or oil. Open daily 10:00–20:00 at 3F First Building, 3-4 Wakamatsu-cho, Yokosuka.
How much does a massage cost at CoconeRu.?
Body care is ¥6,600 for 40 minutes, or ¥4,500 for the quiet version with minimal conversation. Body 30 min plus dry head 30 min is ¥5,980 and is the most popular choice. A first-visit set is ¥4,980. Dry head spa alone runs from ¥4,400 to ¥8,800. All prices include tax, and there is no tipping in Japan. See the full price list.
Do you accept English-speaking customers?
Yes. Our staff speak mainly Japanese and use a translation app with English-speaking guests, which works well in practice. Booking online is easiest because you pick the menu and time on screen without a phone call. If you would rather have a session with almost no talking, book the Quiet Body Care menu.
Do you take credit cards or US dollars?
We accept credit cards, cash in Japanese yen, and QR payments such as PayPay. We cannot accept US dollars, so bring yen if you plan to pay in cash. Tipping is not customary in Japan and is never expected.
Do you offer personal training or pilates in Yokosuka?
Yes. CoconeRu. Move is a private, one-to-one personal training and pilates studio at 2F Yashima Building, 2-11 Otaki-cho, a 4-minute walk from Yokosuka-chuo Station. Sessions combine machine (reformer) and mat pilates with hands-on conditioning for posture, stiff shoulders and a lasting exercise habit. Beginners and both men and women are welcome, and a first-visit trial is available.
