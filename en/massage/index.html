What we do

Body massage and Japanese dry head spa

CoconeRu. is a small relaxation salon a few minutes from Yokosuka-chuo Station. Two things happen here. Body care is hands-on work through the neck, shoulders and back — the ordinary massage most people are looking for after a long week. Dry head spa is a Japanese speciality: a head and scalp massage given with no water, no shampoo and no oil, so your hair stays dry and you can walk straight back out into your day. Most guests book the two together. This is relaxation, not medical or clinical treatment.

Good to know

Before you book

The short version, so nothing is a surprise on the day.

Language

Our staff speak mainly Japanese and use a translation app with English-speaking guests. In practice this works well. Booking online is the easiest route because you just pick a menu and a time — no phone call needed. If you would rather not talk at all, book Quiet Body Care.

Payment

Credit cards, cash in Japanese yen, and QR payments such as PayPay. We cannot accept US dollars. Tipping is not customary in Japan and is never expected.

Who comes here

Women and men are both welcome, and roughly one in five of our guests is a man. You can ask for a male or female practitioner when you book. Appointment only.

What to expect

You stay clothed throughout. For the dry head spa your hair is not washed or wetted, so there is nothing to dry afterwards. Come as you are; there is nothing you need to bring.

More detail on the first visit page. CoconeRu. offers relaxation, not medical or clinical treatment — if something has been bothering you for a long time, please see a doctor.
