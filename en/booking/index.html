Booking

Book online, in English

No phone call needed. Pick a menu and a time below and you are done. We are open every day from 10:00 to 20:00. If you are not sure what to choose, Body 30 min + Dry Head 30 min (¥5,980) is what most people book first — the full price list has everything else.

Head Spa

Massage, dry head spa and facial

3F First Building, 3-4 Wakamatsu-cho, Yokosuka. About 15 minutes on foot from Womble Gate, or 4 minutes from Keikyu Yokosuka-chuo Station.

Move

Personal training and pilates

2F Yashima Building, 2-11 Otaki-cho, Yokosuka. A separate address a few minutes from Head Spa, so check which one you are booked into before you set off.

Good to know

Before you book

The essentials, so nothing is a surprise on the day.

Language

Booking here needs no Japanese at all. In the room, our staff speak mainly Japanese and use a translation app, which works well in practice. If you would rather not talk, book Quiet Body Care.

Payment

Credit cards, cash in Japanese yen, and QR payments such as PayPay. We cannot accept US dollars. Tipping is not customary in Japan and is never expected.

If the form will not load

Some networks block embedded booking forms. Open Head Spa booking or Move booking directly instead, or call 090-7186-4738.

Changing your plans

Please call 090-7186-4738 as early as you can so the slot can go to someone else. More detail on the first visit page.
