Orientation

Where we are

Everything sits in the Yokosuka-chuo area, the downtown strip just outside the base. If you know More’s City, the Daiei building or the covered shopping street, you are already in the right neighbourhood. Note that we run two separate addresses a few minutes apart: the Head Spa for massage and head spa, and Move for personal training and pilates. Check which one you are booked into before you set off.

On foot

Walking from Womble Gate

Womble Gate is the gate closest to downtown, so this is the simplest route for most people coming off base.

Walking

Head out of Womble Gate towards Keikyu Yokosuka-chuo Station, roughly 10 minutes on foot. From the station it is another 4 minutes to either studio. Call it 15 minutes door to door at an easy pace.

By taxi

A few minutes from the gate. Taxi drivers here rarely speak English, so show them the Japanese address below rather than trying to say the street name.

By train

Yokosuka-chuo is on the Keikyu Line. From Shioiri it is one stop. Take the Central exit, and both studios are about 4 minutes away.

Parking

We have no car park of our own, but there are several coin parking lots within a short walk in the Yokosuka-chuo area.
